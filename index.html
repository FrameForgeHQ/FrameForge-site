05 / FAQs

THE DETAILS.

Do I have to provide the footage?

Yes. FRAMEFORGE is an editing service. You supply footage you own or have permission to use.

Do you post videos to my accounts?

No. We supply finished files so you can upload them to your own channels.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Starter and Growth include one revision per video; Premium includes two. Extra revisions can be quoted separately.

How fast will the edits be ready?

We agree a realistic turnaround after reviewing the amount and type of footage.
